# Product vision

Research is difficult to discover when people, projects and outputs live on isolated pages. This hub connects research areas, researchers, projects and publications, with events, opportunities, grants and partners as future extensions.

Discover → Understand → Connect → Participate means finding an idea, understanding its relevance, seeing the people and work behind it, and taking a useful next step. Pages answer: Where am I? Why does this matter? What can I do next?

Public visitors browse without an account. Students will express interest in future participation work. Researchers submit outputs and follow review progress. Administrators curate institutional records and control publication. The long-term value is a trustworthy, current institutional research network rather than a collection of announcements. No invented achievements are represented as genuine Islington records.
